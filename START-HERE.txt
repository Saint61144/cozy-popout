COZY DESKTOP 4.3.2

Install the included extension folder from your browser’s Extensions page
using Developer mode and Load unpacked. The separate drop-in ZIP contains
just the extension. If you have an older version, replace it and refresh YouTube.

Open CozyDesktop.exe, select your browser and press Connect.
Leave the connection tab open. Click the small Reiner on your desktop for
playback controls. Click outside the panel, click Reiner again, or press Escape
to close it. Drag Reiner to move him. Right-click for setup or to exit.
Use Float video on YouTube to pop out the video.

If Connect opens a tab in the wrong browser profile, switch to the profile
with Cozy installed and press Connect in Cozy’s toolbar menu.

Keep the extracted folder. After moving it, press Connect again.
To remove the local link, right-click Reiner, choose Connect, then select
Remove connection. Close the connection tab to end an active link.

The extension uses no service worker. Share the original ZIP because the
generated Cozy.NativeHost.json contains your local installation path.
